# Meher Basan Bhandar --- Design Requirements Document (DRD)

**Version:** 1.0\
**Platforms:** Android and Web\
**Design reference:** Invox --- AI Invoice Management, Dribbble\
**Design principle:** Minimal, premium, clear, and optimized for retail
billing speed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Design vision

Create a modern retail billing application that feels like a polished
financial product while remaining practical for a busy physical shop.

Use the linked Invox design as visual inspiration for: - Spacious
layouts. - Clear information hierarchy. - Restrained colour. - Clean
cards and lists. - Strong typography. - Simple financial summaries.

Do not reproduce the reference screen pixel-for-pixel or carry over
irrelevant AI invoice features.

## 2. Design principles

1.  **Speed first:** New Bill must be reachable immediately.
2.  **Clarity:** Amounts, dues, payment status, and tax must be easy to
    understand.
3.  **Consistency:** Android and Web share the same design language.
4.  **Responsive:** Desktop should use available space; mobile should
    remain touch-friendly.
5.  **Low visual noise:** Avoid unnecessary cards, gradients, shadows,
    and animation.
6.  **Financial trust:** Confirm destructive actions and show clear
    transaction states.
7.  **Accessible:** Readable text, strong contrast, clear focus states,
    and large touch targets.

## 3. Visual design system

### 3.1 Colour tokens

  Token            Light theme   Purpose
  ---------------- ------------- -----------------------------------------
  Primary          `#0625FA`     Primary actions and selected navigation
  Background       `#F5F5F2`     Main app background
  Surface          `#FFFFFF`     Cards, dialogs, panels
  Text primary     `#0A0505`     Main text
  Text secondary   `#6B7280`     Supporting text
  Success          `#3D5835`     Paid, completed, synchronized
  Warning          `#E1522C`     Pending, due, attention
  Border           `#E6E7EB`     Dividers and outlines

Dark theme must define equivalent semantic tokens rather than simply
invert every colour. Verify contrast for all states.

### 3.2 Typography

-   Primary font: Inter, with a suitable system fallback.
-   Use a clear type scale for page title, section title, body, label,
    caption, and financial amount.
-   Use tabular numerals for totals, balances, and report values.
-   Avoid excessive font weights and all-caps labels.

### 3.3 Spacing and shape

-   Use a consistent 4-point spacing base.
-   Prefer 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24-point spacing increments.
-   Cards: medium rounded corners, restrained borders.
-   Inputs: clear labels, visible focus, useful validation text.
-   Buttons: consistent height and distinct primary/secondary hierarchy.
-   Shadows should be subtle and used sparingly.

### 3.4 Icons

-   Use a consistent Lucide-style icon set.
-   Icons must have labels where meaning may be ambiguous.
-   Do not use emoji as functional navigation icons.

## 4. Navigation and information architecture

### Android

Use a bottom navigation bar with five primary destinations: 1. Home 2.
Invoices 3. New Bill (visually prominent) 4. Customers 5. More

The More area contains Payments, Ledger, Reports, Cash Register, and
Settings.

### Web

Use a collapsible left sidebar: - Dashboard - New Bill - Invoices -
Customers - Payments - Customer Ledger - Reports - Cash Register -
Settings

Keep the primary content area spacious. On narrower widths, collapse the
sidebar and adapt tables into compact cards or horizontally scrollable
regions only where necessary.

## 5. Required screens and layouts

### 5.1 Login

-   Business identity and logo.
-   Email/password login.
-   Clear validation and error states.
-   Optional app PIN after authentication.
-   Avoid displaying sensitive account information.

### 5.2 Dashboard

-   Greeting and business name.
-   Main Today's Sales card.
-   Collections and outstanding summaries.
-   Quick actions.
-   Sales chart with date range.
-   Recent invoices list.
-   Clear empty state for a new shop.
-   Loading, error, and offline states.

### 5.3 New Bill

This is the most important screen.

Required hierarchy: 1. Invoice type: GST / Non-GST. 2. Customer selector
with walk-in option. 3. Item-entry rows: description, quantity, unit
label, rate, optional HSN and tax rate. 4. Add item action. 5. Discount
controls. 6. Subtotal, tax breakdown, and grand total. 7. Payment method
and amount received. 8. Due amount. 9. Save, Save & Print, and Share
actions.

Mobile: - Single-column flow. - Sticky or clearly visible grand total
and primary save action. - Numeric keyboard for quantity, rate,
discount, and payment amount. - Avoid cramped multi-column item rows.

Web: - Use a wider item table and a summary panel. - Keep customer and
invoice details accessible without hiding the total. - Support keyboard
navigation and sensible tab order.

### 5.4 Invoice list

-   Search by invoice number or customer.
-   Filter by date and status.
-   Display invoice number, customer, date, total, paid amount, due, and
    status.
-   Use clear Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid, Cancelled, and Quotation
    states.
-   Provide a useful empty state.

### 5.5 Invoice detail

-   Show saved invoice snapshot.
-   Show payment history and outstanding balance.
-   Actions: PDF, Print, Share, Record Payment, and permitted
    Edit/Cancel.
-   Cancellation must show confirmation and reason.
-   Show audit history for important changes.

### 5.6 Customer list and profile

-   Searchable customer list.
-   Customer name, phone, and due indicator.
-   Profile with contact details, invoice history, ledger, and
    outstanding total.
-   Customer groups should be easy to filter.

### 5.7 Payments

-   Payment history with date, customer, invoice, method, amount, and
    reference.
-   Record payment form.
-   Split-payment entry with clear total reconciliation.
-   Payment correction must use an auditable reversal/adjustment.

### 5.8 Customer ledger

-   Chronological debit/credit entries.
-   Running balance.
-   Opening balance only if explicitly supported and documented.
-   Filters by date and invoice.
-   Export/print ledger.

### 5.9 Reports

-   Date range selector.
-   Sales summary cards.
-   Payment method breakdown.
-   Outstanding report.
-   GST summary.
-   Product-description sales report.
-   PDF/Excel export.
-   Clearly label reports unavailable because source data is excluded,
    such as stock valuation and profit.

### 5.10 Cash register

-   Open session and opening cash.
-   Cash in/out entries.
-   Expected cash.
-   Physical counted cash.
-   Difference.
-   Close session with confirmation.

### 5.11 Settings

-   Business profile.
-   GST details.
-   Invoice format and numbering.
-   Theme.
-   Printer preferences.
-   Backup/export/restore.
-   PIN lock.
-   Sync status.

## 6. Component requirements

Build reusable components: - AppScaffold - WebSidebar -
MobileBottomNavigation - PageHeader - MetricCard - PrimaryButton /
SecondaryButton - SearchField - CustomerSelector - InvoiceLineEditor -
MoneyInput - TaxBreakdown - PaymentMethodSelector - PaymentStatusChip -
InvoiceListTile / InvoiceTable - EmptyState - LoadingState -
ErrorState - ConfirmationDialog - DateRangeSelector -
SyncStatusIndicator

Components must receive data and callbacks rather than contain business
logic.

## 7. Interaction and feedback

-   Use immediate inline validation for invalid quantities, rates,
    discounts, and payment amounts.
-   Confirm invoice cancellation and cash-session closure.
-   Show save/sync status without blocking normal work unnecessarily.
-   Disable duplicate submission while an operation is in progress.
-   Preserve unsaved bill data when navigating only if safe and clearly
    indicated.
-   Provide clear success and failure messages.
-   Use skeletons or simple loading indicators for asynchronous content.
-   Respect reduced-motion preferences where supported.

## 8. Responsive breakpoints

Use adaptive layouts rather than fixed screen sizes: - Compact: phone. -
Medium: tablet and narrow browser. - Expanded: desktop browser.

Do not assume a fixed device width. Test portrait and landscape Android
layouts, and common desktop browser widths.

## 9. Accessibility and quality

-   All controls must have accessible labels.
-   Maintain readable contrast in both themes.
-   Support keyboard navigation on Web.
-   Focus order must follow the visual flow.
-   Avoid colour-only status communication; include text labels.
-   Ensure dialogs are usable with keyboard and screen readers.
-   Test large text and browser zoom.

## 10. Design acceptance criteria

-   All screens follow shared spacing, typography, colour, and component
    rules.
-   New Bill is the most prominent workflow.
-   The same invoice is visually consistent across Android, Web, PDF,
    and print.
-   Mobile layouts do not overflow horizontally.
-   Web tables remain readable and usable.
-   Empty, loading, error, offline, and success states are designed.
-   Dark theme has independently verified contrast.
-   No mock values appear in production screens.
